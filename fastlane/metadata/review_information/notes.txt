APP NAME CHANGE
This version renames the app from "ColorLog" (컬러로그) to "ColorBear" (칼라베어). The bundle ID and all features are the same app; the new version adds a watercolor bear character.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
ColorBear is a mood diary that records feelings as colors instead of words. Three times a day (morning, afternoon, evening) a local reminder asks how the user feels, and the user answers by picking one color from the current palette (three to seven colors). Each day appears as a watercolor bear painted in that day's colors, on a grid centered on today, so mood patterns over days and weeks become visible at a glance. It is for people who want to reflect on their feelings but find writing a daily journal burdensome.

HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES
1. Launch the app. Today is the slightly larger bear with a soft glow in the center of the grid (five bears per row). If nothing is recorded yet, today's bear sits uncolored and breathes slowly.
2. Tap today's bear. A sheet opens with the date at the top, a large bear in the middle, three slots (sunrise = morning, sun = afternoon, moon = evening), and a row of the palette's colors at the bottom.
3. Tap a color to record it in the selected slot; the paint spreads over the bear and the next empty slot is selected. Tap a different slot to change that time of day. Tapping the color already in a slot clears it.
4. When all three slots of today are filled, the sheet closes and a short celebration shows the bear cheering. Tap anywhere to dismiss it.
5. Each recorded day gets a randomly chosen bear pose. On a day that has at least one color, tap the large bear in that sheet: the color row is replaced by a row of all 35 bears in that day's colors; tap one to change the pose. Long-pressing a colored bear in the grid opens the same row as a shortcut.
6. Tap the small colored dots at the top right to open Settings. There you can switch among color palettes, set the three reminder times, and allow notifications with the bell button.
   - Tap the dashed + card at the end of the palette list to create a new palette; an editor opens with a live bear preview, a hue slider, a depth slider, and the system color picker. Tap + in the color row to add a color (up to seven); long-press a color to remove it (at least three).
   - Long-press any palette card for a menu: recolor, duplicate, restore original colors (edited built-in palettes), or delete (user-made palettes, with a confirmation). Deleting a palette does not change days that were already recorded.
7. Home Screen widget: long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, and add "오늘의 색" (ColorBear). At each reminder time the widget shows the palette's colors around the bear; tapping one records it without opening the app.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network connections and uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, authentication, payment, or AI services. All data is stored only on the device (SwiftData in an App Group container shared with the widget). Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. The bundled Gaegu font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. The interface is almost entirely colors, bears, and symbols; the few text elements (the date, notification text, widget name) are in Korean in every region.
