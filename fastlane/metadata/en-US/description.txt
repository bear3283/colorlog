ColorLog is a mood diary you write in color, not words.
Even on days when your feelings are hard to put into words, you can still pick a color. Three times a day, a reminder asks how you feel — answer with one color. It takes ten seconds.

Over days and weeks, your colors show the flow of your moods at a glance. No writing, just a simple habit of checking in with yourself.

■ Mood check-ins, three times a day
Morning, afternoon, and evening, a reminder asks: "What color is right now?" Change the times or turn them off whenever you like.

■ A mood tracker that takes one tap
Tap a day and five colors open around it. Pick one and you're done — journaling without the pressure of writing.

■ Edit morning, afternoon, or evening
Choose a time of day with the sunrise, sun, and moon, then change just that color. Tap the same color again to clear it.

■ See your mood over time
Today sits in the middle, with past days stacking up above. The more you log, the more your color diary grows.

■ Log right from your Home Screen widget
At each reminder time, five colors appear on the widget. Pick one without opening the app, and the widget fills with today's color.

■ Nine color palettes
Spring, Dried Flower, Cloud, Jewel, and more — change the colors you log and the mood of the whole app.

■ A sky that follows your day
The background shifts with the time of day: rose at dawn, clear blue at noon, amber at sunset, deep blue at night.

■ Your entries stay on your device
No account, no sign-in, no ads. Everything is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere.

· want a light way to track your mood through the day
· want a small habit of reflecting on how you feel
